Privacy Policy — RR

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Purpose and access

RR is a personal configuration of the open-source rclone application. When the owner grants Google OAuth permission, rclone may list, read, create, modify, and delete Google Drive files as directed by the owner, according to the scope granted.

Use of Google user data

Google Drive data is used only to perform owner-initiated or owner-configured file operations, such as backup and synchronization. RR does not use this data for advertising, profiling, or sale.

Storage

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration on their own devices. Files transferred by rclone are stored only at the storage locations selected by the owner. This website does not accept or store Google account information, tokens, or Drive files.

Sharing and disclosure

There is no application-operated public backend that collects or redistributes Google user data. The owner may configure rclone to move files to another destination; those transfers occur only under the owner's control. Data is not sold or provided to third parties for marketing.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention and deletion of locally stored credentials, files, and backups. Google access can be revoked through Google Account permissions; local tokens can be removed from the owner's rclone configuration.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker (when enabled).
